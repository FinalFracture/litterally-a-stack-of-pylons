# Road strategy lab

Open `scenes/road_strategy_lab.tscn` and press **F6**. F5 still opens Twin Falls.

One road, on the old seed-`20261008` height field. The shoulder is strategy 11 only: a 2 m flat bench, then a 4 m linear blend into the ground. The driving surface is the height field. There is no junction disk.

The path is one course:

- an S-curve that climbs
- a west hairpin, then a bypass that merges back
- a T spur off the hairpin
- a second hairpin and a climb in the southeast
- a six-leg junction: the main road, a north-south road, a diagonal, and a short fork
- a short spur and a Y

WASD slides the cone. The mouse looks. R restarts at the beginning. Esc frees the mouse.

Black is the road, tan is fill, dark brown is cut, red is ground above the road, blue is a missing road. The yellow line is the centre stripe.
