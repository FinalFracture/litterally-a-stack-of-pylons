## Processing
- **Loops:** I cut a segment of length L, then crossfaded the first X seconds with the audio that follows the segment (`out = seg[0:L]`, with `out[0:X] = seg[0:X]·fade_in + seg[L:L+X]·fade_out`). Because of this the end flows into the start exactly as in the original recording, and no audio is reversed or repeated. Beds use X = 3 s equal-power, spots 1–2 s, engines 0.25–0.3 s, and the crossing bell 0.25 s linear (aligned on its strike period). For engines, L was first adjusted (±0.15 s) to the best cyclic match by correlating 0.5 s windows of the low-passed signal. The crossfade is **linear** when that match is strong (corr > 0.6, i.e. phase-coherent) and equal-power otherwise. On every loop the final wrap sample was then moved within ±3 ms to where the source's own sample step was smallest.
- **Levels:** beds target −20 dBFS RMS and spots and engines −16 dBFS RMS, with peaks capped at −1.5 dBFS. One-shots are peak-normalized to −1.5 dBFS. The birds bed is crest-limited (RMS −29.9 at the peak cap), so it is quieter on purpose and its suggested volume is higher.
- **Format:** all files are 48 kHz Ogg Vorbis (q5 beds, q6 others). Beds are stereo; positional emitters, engines, birds and rail are mono. Wind gusts are stereo for use as a non-positional layer.

## Suggested relative mix (beds, starting points; tune by ear)
City −6 dB · suburb −8 · farmland −8 · bird chorus −4 (layer) · wind light −10 (layer everywhere outdoors) · wind strong −8 (replaces light at height or in storms) · distant traffic −12 (layer within ~300 m of highways) · crowd −10 (plazas/events) · lake −8 · river −8. With the Ambience bus at 0 dB, the beds sit about 10–14 dB under the m1 SFX (impacts peak-normalized at −1.5 dBFS).

## Integration note (for the Developer)
**Buses** (the repo has no `default_bus_layout.tres` yet): `Master` ← `Music`, `SFX` (existing `Sfx` autoload, MAX_VOICES 16), **`Ambience`**, **`Engines`**. Give `Ambience` a gentle low-shelf/HP at about 60 Hz and keep it unducked. Duck it about 3 dB under big crashes if wanted. For Quest, a light Reverb only on `SFX`, or none.

**Beds (non-positional):** one `AmbienceDirector` node with **two** `AudioStreamPlayer`s per layer (A/B) for crossfades. Each biome region is an `Area3D` (box/convex shapes: city core, suburb, farmland, lakeshore, riverside, rail corridor) carrying exported `bed`, `volume_db` and `layers` (e.g. suburb = suburb bed + birds bed + wind light). On `body_entered` of the player or cone stack, tween the old bed to −60 dB and the new one to its volume_db over 3 s. Use priority for overlapping zones; the highest priority or most recently entered wins. Start each bed at `play(randf() * length)` so revisits don't always sound identical. Distance-based layers (traffic near highways, wind strong by player height above ground, e.g. linear from 30 m to 120 m) are simple volume curves updated at about 5 Hz, not per frame.

**Positional emitters:** `AudioStreamPlayer3D` for `spot_*` (shore/river points every 40–60 m along water, only the nearest 2–3 active), rail (bell at crossings while gates are down; wheels clack on the train; distant pass/horn on scheduled trains), and birds. Birds: a timer every 4–15 s spawns one random species at a random point 10–40 m from the listener, picking species by biome (robin/cardinal/blue jay/mourning dove/sparrow in suburb, red-winged blackbird/geese/crow on farmland and water). Keep it to at most 2 bird voices at once. Attenuation: `ATTENUATION_INVERSE_DISTANCE`, unit_size 6–10, max_distance 60–120 m. Use logarithmic attenuation with unit_size 30 / max_distance 600 m for train horn and pass. Set `doppler_tracking` = physics on train and vehicle emitters only.

**Engine pitch by speed:** each vehicle gets one `AudioStreamPlayer3D` per layer, idle and drive (cars, scooter). For truck and bus only the idle loop exists, so pitch it up. Map `rpm01 = clamp(speed / top_speed + throttle*0.3, 0, 1)`. Idle layer: pitch_scale `lerp(1.0, 1.6, rpm01)`, volume fades out from rpm01 0.25 to 0.5. Drive layer (recorded at about 2× idle pitch): pitch_scale `lerp(0.7, 1.5, rpm01)`, fades in from 0.2 to 0.45. Truck/bus idle-only: pitch `lerp(1.0, 1.9, rpm01)` plus volume +4 dB with throttle. EV / e-scooter whine: pitch `lerp(0.6, 2.2, speed01)`, volume `linear_to_db(0.2 + 0.8*speed01)`. Smooth rpm01 with `lerp(prev, target, 1 - exp(-8*delta))` to avoid zipper noise. Ambient AI traffic can share one idle loop per vehicle class with random pitch 0.9–1.1 so loops don't phase.

**Quest voice budget:** Godot mixes in software. Plan on about 24 simultaneous voices on Quest: 2–4 beds (only 2 during crossfades), at most 3 spots, at most 2 birds, at most 6 engines (nearest vehicles only; cull beyond 70 m, re-trigger with random offset), plus about 10 SFX via the `Sfx` autoload. Ogg Vorbis decode is the main CPU cost, so keep beds as Ogg and consider importing the short engine and one-shot loops as WAV (QOA) on Quest for cheaper decode. On PC 48+ voices are fine.
